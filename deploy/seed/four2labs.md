## How We Work, a simple, friendly process
You don't need to know any tech to work with us. We translate your goals into a clear plan, then build it.
1. **We listen**, a relaxed chat about your business, your customers, and what's slowing you down.
2. **We plan**, a clear, jargon-free plan with what we'll build, what it does for you, and the timeline.
3. **We build**, our team designs and builds it, sharing progress regularly so there are no surprises.
